# Limb curriculum and imagined proprioception, version 2

Written before collecting data or training this version. Original goal/roll/sit models and results remain frozen. This is a bounded exploratory simulation study, not a claim of successful get-up or human-like consciousness.

Hypothesis: learning single left/right arms and legs, then paired arms, paired legs and all four limbs, with replay of earlier stages, can learn action/force/body-motion relations. Predicted motor effort, contact force and support impulse feed a second attention computation that predicts motion. Group attention permits cross-part interactions; attention weights alone do not establish causal reasoning.

Physics distinction: internal limb motion redistributes segment positions and angular momentum. Whole-body centre-of-mass acceleration follows gravity plus external contact forces divided by total mass. A suspended active limb can indirectly change forces at other supported parts. The network therefore predicts support at all body nodes; "suspended" never means the whole robot is in free flight.

Data: flat-ground floating-base MuJoCo Pi Plus; existing bounded torque/lag/velocity-envelope actuators; no root assistance. Supine/prone passive starts, training seeds 1100–1105, validation 1110–1111, prediction test 1120–1121. Five physically reached anchors per start. At each anchor collect one zero-increment baseline and two random action sequences per group: individual left/right arms, individual left/right legs, both arms, both legs, all limbs. Three actions per sequence, each 0.32 s. A branch starts from the same complete physical state as its baseline; one random successor supplies the next anchor. No joint-motion template or successful get-up demonstration.

Test additionally includes diagonal combinations left arm/right leg and right arm/left leg that never appear as the only active groups in training. Initial-state splits are disjoint, branches within a case are correlated. Airborne/support labels are measured from actual contacts, not inferred from limb names. Nonselected joints remain under the same position controller; they can move and exert holding torque.

Record endpoint proprioception, full-body CoM/velocity, body-part positions, signed mean and RMS motor torque, positive mechanical work, endpoint contact/reaction forces, and per-body external force impulse J and time-weighted impulse K. Check dv = gravity*T + sum(J)/mass and dc = v*T + gravity*T^2/2 + sum(K)/mass, reporting numerical residuals. Effort is integral sum((actual torque / torque limit)^2)dt, not electrical energy or biological fatigue.

Architecture: URDF-consistent forward kinematics; body, ground and global tokens; explicit limb tokens with cross-part attention; predicted sensation tokens attend back into motion prediction. Whole-body CoM translation and velocity use the predicted external impulses and Newton's law, while internal motion changes the mass-weighted body geometry. Force/impulse labels supervise training only; future measured force is never a policy input. Use multi-step autoregressive training and three-step (0.96 s) imagination. This horizon is still short for complete get-up.

Main comparison: curriculum vs globally shuffled order of exactly the same training sample presentations, model initialization and optimizer budget. Compare the final checkpoints at the same update count; validation curves are diagnostic and test data never select checkpoints. Also inspect sensory-path interventions at evaluation as diagnostics, not as a separately trained causal ablation. Report per-group and measured contact-mode prediction errors, errors on inactive body parts, CoM motion/velocity, torque RMS, contact forces/impulses and effort ranking. Compare zero-motion / constant-current-force baselines. Freeze source/checkpoint hashes before inspecting prediction-test outcomes.

Train a shared goal-conditioned attention proposal for three-action sequences using physically achieved body-point goals and the same curriculum. Planning evaluates proposals and bounded random sequences through the learned model only, then selects a goal-relevant sequence with or without a predicted effort preference. Validate chosen actions in real physics. All goals and comparison methods use identical candidates and execution duration. Energy comparison must be paired at comparable goal achievement; doing nothing or failing is not an efficiency success. All unsuccessful control trials are retained. No claim that this establishes walking, rolling or sitting up.

Before reporting: check mask isolation, train/validation/test separation, exact sample-presentation matching, physical impulse consistency, future-sensation exclusion, frozen previous files, and replay at least one selected physical sequence. Publish data coverage gaps and negative results.

Details fixed before prediction-test inspection: train both world models for 320 updates, batch 96, 80 updates per curriculum stage, 35% old-stage replay. Train the three-action goal proposal for 120 updates with batch 64, position goals using full coordinates or vertical-only masks. Composition validation uses 16 paired sets (supine/prone seeds 1200/1201 × arms/legs/four limbs/diagonal), each with hold/A/B/A+B from exactly the same initial state. These physical counterfactuals are diagnostics only. Closed-loop goals: raise either wrist or foot by 15 mm, supine/prone seeds 1200/1201, 16 goal cases per method. Methods: actor alone, learned-model goal selection, learned-model goal selection with effort preference; 64 candidates, three-action imagination, execute only the first action before re-observing. Fixed six control blocks plus two common hold blocks (2.56 s total); final 16 samples span 0.30 s, with height error <=8 mm and target-point speed <=0.04 m/s throughout. Retain all 48 trials, and report effort only in pairs where both planning variants succeed.

Validation-only amendment, before any test predictions: the first version extrapolated current per-node forces to initialize average impulses. Validation CoM RMSE was about 76 mm at one step and 162 mm at three steps for curriculum, versus a 6/15 mm stationary baseline. Sources, checkpoints and logs are retained in `schema_v2_results/prototype_v1/`. Reparameterize impulse as predicted total resultant (nominal gravity-balanced support) plus learned per-body normal load fractions and opposing shear contributions. Each local impulse respects the friction cone; sums equal the predicted resultant. Newton's CoM equations remain exact for these predicted impulses. A soft loss penalizes invalid time-weighted vertical impulse > T*ordinary impulse; this is not a hard contact-trajectory feasibility guarantee. Increase torque RMS/effort supervision weights. Both models are reinitialized and retrained with the original identical sample multisets and update budgets. The supported-body prior has not been validated for whole-body free flight.
